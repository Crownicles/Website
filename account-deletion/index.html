Delete your account

Last updated: 22 September 2026

See our Privacy Policy
Table of contents
From the app By writing to us What is deleted What is kept Timing Contact us

From the app

The request is made from the app settings, under "Delete my account". A screen sums up what disappears and what remains.

The request is then reviewed, to check that it comes from the account holder. A confirmation code is then sent to the email address attached to the account, within a few days.

Entering that code, in the same screen of the app, triggers the deletion. Without that confirmation nothing is erased: we cannot give it on your behalf.

By writing to us

If the app is not installed, deletion may be requested at contact@crownicles.com, either from the email address attached to the account or by stating the username concerned.

The confirmation code is sent to you the same way. It can be sent back to the Discord bot in a private message, or entered in the app.

What is deleted

The account and everything that identifies you are erased: identifier, email address, username, password, and any link with a Discord account.

Signing in becomes impossible, whether from the app, from Discord, or from the website.

What is kept

Game data is kept in an anonymous form once the account is erased: nothing remains that links it to you as a person. The character stays visible in the leaderboards under an anonymous name.

Free-text entries you made, such as the names given to pets, a guild name and its description, are kept as well. They serve game balancing and the consistency of other players' games.

Deletion of that content may be requested separately, at the same address.

Timing

Deletion is triggered as soon as the confirmation code is entered. It is immediate and irreversible: progress cannot be restored.

Reviewing the request, then sending the code, takes a few days. The whole process stays within one month, in line with the General Data Protection Regulation.

Contact us

For any question about deleting your account, you can write to us at:

contact@crownicles.com
