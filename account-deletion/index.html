Delete your account

From the app

The Crownicles app lets you delete your account without going through us. The path is in the settings, under "Delete my account".

A screen sums up what disappears and what remains, then asks for a confirmation. Deletion happens immediately after that confirmation.

By writing to us

If the app is not installed, deletion may be requested at contact@crownicles.com, either from the email address attached to the account or by stating the username concerned.

A personal code is then sent to you by email. That code must be sent back to the Discord bot in a private message, then confirmed with a phrase: deletion is triggered by that confirmation alone, which we cannot give on your behalf.

What is deleted

The account and everything that identifies you are erased: identifier, email address, username, password, and any link with a Discord account.

Signing in becomes impossible, whether from the app, from Discord, or from the website.

What is kept

Game data is kept in an anonymous form once the account is erased: nothing remains that links it to you as a person. The character stays visible in the leaderboards under an anonymous name.

Free-text entries you made, such as the names given to pets, a guild name and its description, are kept as well. They serve game balancing and the consistency of other players' games.

Deletion of that content may be requested separately, at the same address.

Timing

Deletion carried out from the app is immediate and irreversible: progress cannot be restored.

A request sent by email is handled within one month, in line with the General Data Protection Regulation. Deletion takes effect as soon as you confirm the code you received.

Contact us

For any question about deleting your account, you can write to us at:

contact@crownicles.com
